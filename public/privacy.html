Privacy

Last updated 17 October 2026

Summary

Pharvel has no accounts and does not store your questions. It keeps only counts that cannot identify you.

What is processed

  • Your question is used to look up the medicines and is not stored.
  • Your IP address is converted into a one-way code to apply the daily limit of free checks and to count distinct visitors. The address itself is not stored. These codes expire within about two days.
  • Counts: how many checks were run, which pairs of medicines are checked most, and how often safety notices are shown. These contain no personal information.
  • Problem reports: if you send one, we keep the reason, the medicines and result shown, and your optional comment (up to 300 characters). Please do not include personal information. The newest 500 reports are kept.
  • Your saved medicine list stays in your own browser and is never sent to us except when you run a check.

Who else receives data

To answer, medicine names (not your identity) are sent to the US National Library of Medicine (RxNorm), the US Food and Drug Administration (openFDA) and the National Center for Biotechnology Information (PubChem). The site is hosted by Vercel and counts are stored with Upstash. If AI-written explanations are enabled in future, the label passages and your question would be sent to Anthropic to produce them; this page will be updated first.

Your choices

You can clear your saved medicine list by removing the medicines or clearing your browser's site data. Because we store nothing that identifies you, we cannot look up or delete records about a specific person.
