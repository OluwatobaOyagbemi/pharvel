FAQ

Good questions.

Is this medical advice?

No. Pharvel shows what drug labels say. It can't know your doses, conditions or other treatments. Ask a pharmacist or doctor before changing anything.

Where does the information come from?

Drug names come from NIH RxNorm. Label text comes from openFDA, and each finding links to the DailyMed label it was quoted from.

What if it finds nothing for a pair?

That only means the labels checked don't mention it. It does not prove the combination is safe.

Does it cost anything?

Not right now. It's free to use.
